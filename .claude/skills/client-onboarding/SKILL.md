# Client onboarding

1. **Ask first.** One offer, one audience, one channel, daily test budget, what counts as success, target cost per lead. If the user does not know the target CPL, leave it empty; do not guess one.
2. **Create the client** in the app (`/clients/new`). The app is the source of truth.
3. **Knowledge base.** Draft each document from what the user gave you and from the client's own website. Mark every statement you could not verify as a question for the user. Never fill gaps with plausible-sounding claims. The user saves the documents in the app under Knowledge.
4. **Customer voice.** Ask for 10 to 15 real questions or objections from customers, without names.
5. **Competitors.** Research 3 to 5 competitors (their websites, Meta Ad Library, Google results). Record their offer and ad angles, with the source. Do not copy their wording into drafts.
6. **Checklist.** Go through the app's Checklist with the user. Landing page, tested conversion tracking and consent come before any ad spend. For the legal items say what needs checking and by whom; do not declare them done.
7. **First ads by hand.** The user launches the first 2 to 3 ads manually. Agent-written drafts start once there is a week of data.
